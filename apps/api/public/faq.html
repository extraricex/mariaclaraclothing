What is Maria Clara Clothing?

A proudly Filipino clothing brand blending classic Filipino elegance with modern everyday streetwear.

Where are your products made?

Our pieces are designed in the Philippines and produced with trusted local partners.

What sizes do you offer?

Most shirts run from Small to 3XLarge. Check each product page before ordering because sold-out sizes are disabled.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery is the featured checkout method for this frontend prototype.

How long does delivery take?

Metro Manila usually takes 2-4 business days. Provincial orders usually take 4-6 business days.
